# Assets

- `paper.webp`, `paper-dark.webp`: the page ground texture, 512 × 512 tiles. Made from the photo `229-plain-white-paper` supplied by the author; its license needs no attribution (confirmed by the author, 2026-10-14). Processing: grayscale, a square crop made seamless (blended with half-tile shifted copies, one axis at a time), lighting removed with a high-pass filter, and reduced to fine, low-contrast grain (at most 5% darker). They display at 320px, so the grain stays fine and sharp on dense screens. CSS blends them into the ground color (`background-blend-mode`: multiply in light mode, screen in dark), so the page keeps its exact color.
- `sponsors/cube-logo-light.svg`, `sponsors/cube-logo-dark.svg`: Cube's logo for light and dark grounds, unchanged from Cube's brand kit (https://cube.dev/brand, downloaded 2026-10-14). Its rules: use the files as provided (no recoloring or effects), the matching file for each ground instead of inverting, clear space of at least the cube mark's width around the logo, and no implied partnership beyond the real one (Cube sponsors Chartist).
