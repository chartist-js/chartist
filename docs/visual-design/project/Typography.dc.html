Why Zilla Slab

Aa Gg Rr 1848

Slab serifs were the headline type of the 1840s press. Clarendon was cut in 1845, in the middle of the Chartist years. Zilla Slab carries that spirit with soft, sturdy shapes that match the 2 px outlines, offset shadows and flat mascot. It belongs to the same family as the UI instead of fighting it.

Rules: display sizes only (22 px and up), weights 600–700, never for body, UI labels or code. Also tried: Grenze (too calligraphic next to the cartoon shadows), Alfa Slab One (too loud), Young Serif (looser press reference).
