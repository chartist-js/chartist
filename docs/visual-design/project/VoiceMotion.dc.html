06 · Motion

Charts draw, they don't pop

1 · Draw in

Lines stroke in over --dur-draw (1200 ms, --ease-out). Series stagger by 150 ms, points pop in after the line arrives, bars grow from the baseline.

2 · Springy small things

Buttons lift 2 px with their offset shadow; points and the hat tip use --ease-spring with a small overshoot.

3 · The mascot reacts

He blinks every few seconds; on hover his hat tips and his moustache wiggles. Never more than one reaction at a time.

4 · One hero moment per page

The landing hero draws the board chart on the easel; docs pages animate only the example in view. Nothing moves while people read.

5 · prefers-reduced-motion

Every animation jumps to its final frame: charts appear fully drawn, loops stop, confetti is hidden, hover lifts become color changes. Handled once, globally, in the stylesheet.

--dur-1: 120ms;  /* hover, press */
--dur-2: 240ms;  /* cards, menus */
--dur-3: 480ms;  /* points, hat tip */
--dur-draw: 1200ms; /* chart lines */
--ease-out: cubic-bezier(.22, 1, .36, 1);
--ease-spring: cubic-bezier(.34, 1.56, .64, 1);
